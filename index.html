How to Play

Celebrity Chain — everyone starts with points. Points are only LOST by making mistakes.

  • The computer picks a starting celebrity. The chain begins from its last name's first letter.
  • Players take turns naming a celebrity whose first name begins with the required letter.
  • After each submission, a 3-second challenge window opens. Any other player can challenge.
  • Challenged and the name is real: challenger loses 1 point.
  • Challenged and the name is fake: submitter loses 1 point, fake name is removed, new seed generated.
  • Pass, timeout, or mistake: lose 1 point.
  • Everyone passes in a row: new seed generated, chain restarts.
  • End Game anytime — the player with the most points wins.
